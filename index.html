Privacy Policy — HirePro

Last updated: November 11, 2026

HirePro ("HirePro", "we", "us") is a mobile app operated by Masha Construction LLC that connects customers with independent contractors ("Pros"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using HirePro you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up we collect your name, email address, phone number, password (stored in encrypted/hashed form), and your role in the app (Customer or Pro).

Profile and Pro verification information. Pros may add a bio, service categories, rates, service area, and profile photo. Pros may also provide license details (license number, state, expiry) and insurance details (provider, policy number, expiry), including uploaded copies of license and insurance documents, so customers can see that a Pro is verified.

Job information. When a Customer requests a quote or books a job, we collect the job title and description, the job address, preferred date, budget, and any photos the Customer attaches to the request.

Messages. Customers and Pros exchange messages inside the app about a job. We store those messages so both sides can follow the conversation and the job history.

Booking and review information. We keep records of bookings and their status (Requested, In discussion, Booked, In progress, Completed), quotes sent by Pros, invoice amounts and whether an invoice was marked paid, and reviews (star ratings and text) written after a completed job.

Location (optional). If you tap the location option and grant permission, the app uses your device's approximate or precise location to show you Pros near you. Your coordinates are sent to our server only to run that "nearby" search. Location access is off unless you grant it, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.

Device and notification data. If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token for your device so we can send alerts about your jobs and messages. We also receive basic technical information needed to run the app (such as device type and app version) and server logs.

What we do not collect. HirePro does not process payments in the app — there is no card or bank payment collection in this version — and we do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in securely.
  • To match Customers with Pros: displaying Pro profiles, categories, ratings, and reviews, and running nearby-Pro searches.
  • To pass job requests, quotes, bookings, and per-job messages between the Customer and the Pro working on that job.
  • To show Pros their leads, pipeline, quotes, and earnings inside the app, including an automated lead-quality summary/score for Pros.
  • To send service notifications (for example, a new message or a booking update) and important account or service emails.
  • To keep the app safe, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with the law.

3. How information is shared

  • Between Customers and Pros: when you request a quote or book a job, the Pro sees the job details you submitted (including the job address and photos) and your name, and you see the Pro's profile, ratings, and verification status. Messages you send in a job chat are visible to the other party in that job.
  • Public profile information: a Pro's public profile (name, photo, bio, categories, service area, ratings, reviews, and verified badges) is visible to other users of the app.
  • Service providers: we use hosting and database providers to run the app, and notification/email delivery providers to send alerts. They process data only to provide those services to us.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

4. Data retention

We keep your account and job information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal, accounting, or safety obligations. Messages, bookings, and reviews connected to completed jobs may be retained after a job ends for record-keeping and dispute resolution.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings/Profile → Delete Account permanently erases your profile and cascade-deletes your jobs, messages, and invoices, subject to any records we must keep by law. Deletion is permanent.
  • Access and correction: you can view and update most profile information directly in the app, or contact us to request a copy or correction of your information.
  • Notifications and location: you can turn off push notifications and location access at any time in your device settings.
  • Email: contact us at the address below for any privacy request.

6. Children

HirePro is a home-services marketplace for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use passwords stored in hashed form, signed login tokens, and encrypted (HTTPS) connections between the app and our servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit internal access to what is needed to operate the service.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email. Continuing to use HirePro after an update means you accept the revised policy.
